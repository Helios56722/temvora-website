# TemVora business card

Open **business-card-proof.html** to review both sides at the 3.5 × 2 inch trim size. **business-card-proof.png** combines both sides, while **front.png** and **back.png** provide individual proofs. The HTML embeds the same EB Garamond font and swirl-O and star wordmark used by the website, so the proof works without a network connection.

The front presents nine TemVora service areas:

- Tech Support
- Business Growth
- Website Design
- Resumes
- Contract Creation
- Business Assistant
- Configuration Management
- Network Engineer
- Marketing

The back carries the TemVora wordmark, contact information, and a QR code for `https://temvora.helios56722.chatgpt.site`. Both sides use the website's deep-midnight background, violet and magenta line work, light-lilac type, and Garamond typography.

The layout was checked at the 336 × 192 CSS-pixel trim: the service grid, wordmark, contact rows, and QR panel do not overlap, and the main text retains strong contrast against the background.

The `output/` folder includes 300-DPI front and back PNGs with 0.125-inch bleed, a two-page print-ready PDF, the QR asset, and a browser proof. Confirm the current contact details, service language, and print specifications before placing an order.
