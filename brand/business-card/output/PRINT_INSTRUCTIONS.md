## Printer specifications

- Finished trim: 3.5 x 2 inches.
- Artwork size: 3.75 x 2.25 inches.
- Bleed: 0.125 inch on every side.
- Resolution: 300 DPI.
- PDF order: page 1 front, page 2 back.
- Print at actual size / 100 percent. Do not use fit-to-page.
- The artwork is RGB. Ask the selected printer to apply its own CMYK profile during preflight.
- Recommended starting stock: 16 pt matte or soft-touch, subject to a physical proof.
